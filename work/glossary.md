# Glossary: naming decisions

The series rule, shared with the PS II, III and IV retranslations: American English, Japanese
names throughout. **PS I names are spelled as the PS IV retranslation ships them** - when a
shared series name comes up, grep `../ps4-translate`'s `en` text (and `../ps2-translate`'s
`work/glossary.md`, which already follows it), not a glossary table. Where modern official
material prints an English form of the katakana, take it (Foie, Resta, ...); otherwise
transliterate, with etymology only settling ambiguous letters.

Nothing below has been checked against the PS I Japanese script yet: the JP column is the
form the PS II glossary records. Every row is provisional until the script is extracted.
